Platform Overview

Universal & Chemistry-Agnostic

TwinVolt decouples battery physics and estimation algorithms from hardware variations. Whether monitoring Li-ion (NMC, LFP) packs, solid-state cells, or custom prototypes, TwinVolt synchronizes real-time sensor streams with state-space models.

Equivalent Circuit Models

Continuous parameter calibration with multi-pair RC Thévenin models to represent polarization and open-circuit voltage dynamics.

Dual State Estimation

Extended Kalman Filtering (EKF) operating alongside Coulomb Counting with drift compensation and covariance propagation.

Explainable Diagnostics

Evidence-based Bayesian fault hypotheses identifying thermal runaway risk, overcharge, dendrite growth, and sensor disconnects.
